Privacy Policy

High Society Club Cebu (High Society Club OPC), 2F Springwood District, Plaridel St., Cambaro, Mandaue City, Cebu, Philippines.

This policy covers messages you send to our Instagram and Facebook accounts, which are answered by our booking assistant (an AI) and our staff.

What we collect

Your Instagram or Facebook name and handle, the messages you send us, and the booking details you give: date, number of guests, name for the booking, contact number, arrival time, guest list, special requests, and the payment screenshot for your deposit.

Why we use it

Only to answer your questions, take and confirm your table booking, check your deposit, and follow up on requests or complaints. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Our staff, and the service providers that run the booking assistant: Meta (Instagram and Facebook messaging), Anthropic (the AI that writes the replies) and our hosting provider. They process it only to provide the service.

Payments

Deposits are paid by scanning our InstaPay QR in your own banking or e-wallet app. Never send card numbers in chat; we do not take card details in messages.

How long we keep it

Booking records are deleted 2 weeks after the booking date.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, as allowed under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. Email raoul2201@gmail.com (raoul2201@gmail.com) or message us on Instagram @HighSocietyCebu.
